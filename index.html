FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything importers, buyers, and sourcing teams ask before they hit the show floor.

  • Is Show Sourcing for iPhone or iPad?

    Both, and Mac too. It is built for one-handed iPhone use on the trade show floor, and works on iPad and Mac for reviewing your catalog. Your team can also sign in to the web app at app.showsourcing.app. iOS 18.0 or later.

  • Does it work offline?

    Yes. Capture products, scan cards, take notes, and manage vendors offline — everything syncs when you're back on WiFi or cellular. Ideal for factory visits and expo halls with spotty signal.

  • What trade shows is it built for?

    Canton Fair, Global Sources, ASD, IHA, Hong Kong Electronics Fair, HKTDC, Ambiente, IFA, CES — any expo where you're scanning vendors and cataloging products at speed. Also for factory visits (Shenzhen, Yiwu, Guangzhou, Vietnam, India).

  • How does the business card scanner work?

    Point your camera at a card, tap capture. AI extracts the company name, contact person, email, phone, WeChat ID, and physical address in about half a second. Multi-card imports supported — scan a stack, get all the vendors back at once.

  • Can multiple people on my team share the same catalog?

    Yes. Real-time team sync means everyone sees new products and vendor cards as they get scanned. Team Radar shows colleague locations on the venue map so you can meet up without calling.

  • How does landed cost calculation work?

    Enter FOB, packaging cost, and container quantity. Show Sourcing applies country-specific tariff rules (auto-linked to the product's category and country of origin) plus your container cost, and gives you the LDP — the true per-unit landed cost you can use to price the item.

  • What does it cost?

    $60 per seat per 6 months, or $102 per seat per year (15% annual discount). Volume discounts kick in at 10+ seats. Billed through the App Store. See Pricing for every plan.

  • How is my data stored?

    Your catalog syncs to a private cloud backend (Supabase, hosted in Singapore for global reach). Team data stays within your team. See the Privacy Policy for details.

  • Is there an Android version?

    Not yet — Android is on the roadmap. The iOS app is the focus.
