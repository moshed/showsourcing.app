Delete Your Account

Show Sourcing by DNZ Consulting LLC · Last updated: November 1, 2026

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Show Sourcing on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  2. Go to Settings and tap your name at the top.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account, then confirm.

Or ask us by email

Send an email from the address you sign in with to general@showsourcing.app with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done. Use this if you are on Android or cannot open the app.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in and profile
  • The products and vendors you created, with their photos and business cards
  • Your comments, notifications and app logs
  • Your paid plan, which we cancel so you are not charged again

What we keep

Products and vendors your teammates created stay with their team. We keep billing records and a short record of account actions only where the law or a payment dispute requires it. See the Privacy Policy.
