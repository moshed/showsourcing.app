Show Sourcing vs Excel and Google Sheets for trade show sourcing

Oct 20, 2026 3 min read By DNZ

Most buyers start with a spreadsheet. It is free, everyone knows it, and it sorts by price. For a small trip it is often enough. It gets hard when a show means hundreds of products, a phone in one hand, and three teammates editing at once.

The short answer

  • Use Excel or Google Sheets if you visit a few booths, buy alone, and mainly need a price list.
  • Use Show Sourcing if you need the photo, the vendor's card, the price, the price breaks and the landed cost to stay together, on a phone, offline, with a team.

Where a spreadsheet wins

  • It is free or already paid for.
  • Total flexibility. Any column, any formula.
  • Everyone can open it, including your customer and your accountant.

Where a spreadsheet hurts at a show

  • Photos and rows drift apart. The photo is in the camera roll, the price is in row 214.
  • Typing on a phone is slow. A booth visit becomes five minutes of data entry.
  • Cards stay as cards. Someone has to type 300 of them after the show.
  • Landed cost needs work. You build and maintain the formulas and tariff rates yourself.

Use both

Show Sourcing exports products and vendors to Excel, with photos in the cells and landed-cost formulas that still work. Capture in the app at the show, and share the spreadsheet afterwards.
