When a spreadsheet is enough

If you visit a handful of booths, buy alone, and only need a price list, a spreadsheet is fine and free. Show Sourcing earns its place when photos, vendors, price breaks and landed cost all need to stay together, and when a team works the same show.

When a general CRM fits better

General CRMs are built around contacts, deals and a sales pipeline. Show Sourcing is built around products: photos, quotes, MOQ, tariffs and landed cost, linked to the vendor who quoted them. It is not a sales CRM.

Questions

What is Show Sourcing?

Show Sourcing records the products you find at trade shows. You take photos, add the vendor and the price, and the app calculates the landed cost. Your team sees every product on their own phones.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. You can create and edit products offline. The app saves them on the phone and uploads them when you are online again. Settings > Data > Sync Queue shows what is still waiting to upload.

How do I export my products?

Settings > Backup & Import > Export Products (JSON). The file has every field and links to every photo. It is not available during the free trial, and the team owner can limit it.

Who sees my products?

Everyone on your team. Products sync to all team phones in a few seconds. People outside your team never see them.

How much does Show Sourcing cost?

You pay per seat (one seat for each person). Yearly is $8.50 a seat a month. 6 months is $10 a seat a month. Teams of 10 or more get a discount. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. See showsourcing.app/pricing.
