Show Sourcing vs CamCard for trade show business cards

Oct 20, 2026 3 min read By DNZ

CamCard is a well-known business card scanner. At a trade show, a card scan is half the job. The other half is remembering what that vendor showed you and what they quoted. Show Sourcing does both.

Facts about CamCard come from camcard.com and its App Store listing, checked on October 20, 2026.

The short answer

  • Choose CamCard if you only need to digitize business cards into contacts, on many platforms, including Android.
  • Choose Show Sourcing if each card is a supplier, and you need their products, photos, prices, MOQ and landed cost linked to them.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a separate business card scanner app at a trade show?

Not if your sourcing app scans cards. Show Sourcing reads the name, company, phones, emails, WeChat, website and address into a vendor record, and links it to the products you capture.

Does CamCard track products and prices?

Its website does not list product or price tracking. It focuses on contacts, card scanning and digital business cards.
