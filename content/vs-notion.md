Notion is a popular workspace for notes, documents and simple databases. Some buyers keep show notes in it. Show Sourcing is built for one job: recording products, vendors and prices at trade shows, and comparing them afterwards.

Facts about Notion come from its pricing page, checked on October 20, 2026. See [notion.com/pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing) for current prices.

## The short answer

- Choose **Notion** for team documents, meeting notes and planning, and for light product lists.
- Choose **Show Sourcing** when you need to capture a product at a booth in under a minute and later compare hundreds of quotes by price, MOQ and landed cost.

## Where Notion is stronger

- **Documents, wikis and planning.** Use it for your show plan and your team's sourcing guidelines.
- **Free plan** for individuals.
- **One place for many kinds of work.**

## Where Show Sourcing is stronger

- **Speed at the booth.** Photo, card scan and price in one step.
- **Real numbers.** Sort and filter by FOB, landed cost, MOQ and status.
- **Landed cost** with US tariffs and freight per unit.
- **Vendors from cards**, with every product they quoted on one page.

## Use both

Plan the trip in Notion. Record the booths in Show Sourcing. Share a Show Sourcing Excel export in your Notion page after the show.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use Notion for trade show sourcing?

Yes, for notes and simple product lists. It does not scan business cards or calculate landed cost, and you build the price fields yourself.

### How much does Notion cost compared with Show Sourcing?

On October 20, 2026, Notion listed Free, Plus at $10 and Business at $20 per member per month. Show Sourcing costs $102 per seat per year or $60 per seat for 6 months.
