CardFlow Expo and Show Sourcing are both made for buyers at trade shows. They start from different places. CardFlow Expo starts from the **supplier**: scan the card, build a supplier profile, follow up. Show Sourcing starts from the **product**: photo, price, MOQ and landed cost, linked to the vendor who quoted it.

Facts about CardFlow Expo come from its own website, checked on October 20, 2026. Plans and prices change, so check [cardflowexpo.com](https://cardflowexpo.com/) for the latest.

## The short answer

- Choose **CardFlow Expo** if you use **Android**, want a free tier, and mostly need supplier contacts, follow-up reminders and per-event pricing.
- Choose **Show Sourcing** if you use **iPhone, iPad or Mac**, and you need product photos, price breaks, landed cost with US tariffs and a team that sees each other's finds live.

## Where CardFlow Expo is stronger

- **Android.** Show Sourcing has no Android app.
- **A free plan** with real limits, and **per-event pricing** for buyers who go to one show a year.
- **More interface languages.**
- **Exhibitor-side tools** such as QR lead forms.

## Where Show Sourcing is stronger

- **Products, not only suppliers.** Every product keeps its photos, video, voice note, price, price breaks and MOQ, linked to the vendor.
- **Landed cost.** Show Sourcing adds packaging, US tariffs by HTS code and freight per unit, so you compare quotes on what they really cost. See [how to compare supplier quotes](/blog/compare-supplier-quotes/).
- **Search inside photos.** Words printed on the product or its box become searchable.
- **Live team sync on every plan**, not only on a business tier.
- **Apple platforms**, including iPad and Mac with list and details side by side.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Show Sourcing work on Android?

No. Show Sourcing runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with a web portal for viewing your catalog. CardFlow Expo lists Android and web.

### Which is cheaper, Show Sourcing or CardFlow Expo?

CardFlow Expo has a free plan and per-event plans from $49. Show Sourcing costs $60 per seat for 6 months or $102 per seat per year, with a 7-day free trial. Which is cheaper depends on how many shows you attend and how many people use it.

### Which one calculates landed cost?

Show Sourcing calculates landed cost (LDP) with US tariffs and freight per unit. CardFlow Expo's website does not list landed cost.
