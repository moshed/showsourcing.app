Airtable is a flexible database that looks like a spreadsheet. Many teams build their own sourcing tracker in it. Show Sourcing is a ready-made sourcing app for trade shows.

Facts about Airtable come from its pricing page, checked on October 20, 2026. See [airtable.com/pricing](https://airtable.com/pricing) for current prices.

## The short answer

- Choose **Airtable** if you want to design your own database, connect it to other tools, and you have time to build and maintain it.
- Choose **Show Sourcing** if you want something that works at the booth today: photo, card scan, price breaks, landed cost and team sync, without building it.

## Side by side

| | Show Sourcing | Airtable |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A sourcing app for trade shows | A general database you configure |
| Setup | Ready to use | You design tables, fields and views |
| Price | $60 per seat for 6 months, or $102 per seat per year ($8.50 a month) | Free plan; Team $20 per user per month and Business $45 per user per month, billed yearly |
| Business card scan | Yes | Not listed on the pricing page |
| Landed cost with US tariffs | Built in | You build formulas and keep tariff rates yourself |
| Works offline on the show floor | Yes | Not listed on the pricing page |
| Photos, video, voice notes per product | Yes | Attachments field; voice transcription not listed |
| Integrations and automations | Excel export, AI Connection for Claude and ChatGPT | Many, a core strength |

## Where Airtable is stronger

- **Flexibility.** It can track anything, beyond sourcing.
- **Integrations and automations** with other business tools.
- **A free plan** for small bases.

## Where Show Sourcing is stronger

- **No building.** The fields buyers need already exist: price term, price breaks, MOQ, packaging, units per container.
- **Landed cost** with US tariffs by HTS code, kept current for you.
- **Business card scanning** straight into vendors.
- **Made for a phone at a booth**, and it works offline.
- **Lower price per seat** than Airtable's paid plans.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use Airtable as a sourcing database?

Yes. Many teams do. You design the tables and fields, and you maintain formulas such as landed cost yourself.

### How much does Airtable cost compared with Show Sourcing?

On October 20, 2026, Airtable listed a free plan, Team at $20 per user per month and Business at $45 per user per month, billed yearly. Show Sourcing costs $102 per seat per year ($8.50 a month) or $60 per seat for 6 months.
