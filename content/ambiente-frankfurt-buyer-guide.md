**Ambiente** is the big consumer goods fair at **Messe Frankfurt** in Germany. It covers tableware, kitchen, home décor and gifts, and draws buyers and suppliers from around the world.

Facts on this page come from ambiente.messefrankfurt.com, checked on 27 October 2026. Ticket prices and hall plans can change, so check the official site.

## The short answer

- **Dates:** **29 January - 2 February 2027**.
- **Venue:** Messe Frankfurt, Frankfurt am Main, Germany.
- **Sections:** **Dining**, **Living** and **Giving**. "Interior Looks" is in Hall 3.1.
- **New for sourcing:** a **Global Sourcing Dining** area for manufacturers.
- **Who can go:** trade visitors only.
- **Tickets:** bought online in the ticket shop. When we checked, an online 1-day ticket was about EUR 29 and a season ticket about EUR 55 (with Info Service). Check current prices.

## What is in each section

| Section | What you find |
|---|---|
| Dining | Tableware, cookware, kitchen tools, table textiles |
| Living | Home décor, furniture accents, home textiles, interior design |
| Giving | Gifts, seasonal decoration, fashion accessories, stationery |

The **Global Sourcing** areas gather manufacturers, many from Asia, who make products to order. That is where to look for private label and custom products.

## Plan your days

Five days and many halls is a lot of walking. A plan saves your feet.

1. **Use the official exhibitor search.** Shortlist by product group and note hall and stand.
2. **Give one day to Global Sourcing** if you buy private label.
3. **Group the rest by section.** Dining halls one day, Living and Giving the next.
4. **Book meetings ahead** with brands you already know.
5. **Use the Business Club lounges** (Hall 4.2 and Hall 11.0 foyers) for meetings, if your ticket includes access.

## At each stand

- Ask the price, the MOQ and the price term (EXW, FOB or delivered).
- For manufacturers in Global Sourcing, ask about custom colors, packaging and lead time.
- For European brands, ask about distribution rights in your country.
- Photograph each product with its stand number, and swap cards.
- See [25 questions to ask suppliers](/blog/questions-to-ask-suppliers-at-trade-shows/).

## After the fair

Ambiente ends just before Chinese New Year slowdowns in many years. Follow up fast with Asian suppliers. See [how to follow up](/blog/follow-up-with-suppliers-after-trade-show/). Compare European and Asian quotes on landed cost, not on price alone. See [landed cost from FOB to LDP](/blog/landed-cost-fob-to-ldp/).

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Ambiente 2027?

Ambiente 2027 runs 29 January - 2 February 2027 at Messe Frankfurt in Frankfurt am Main, Germany.

### Is Ambiente open to the public?

No. The official visitor page says Ambiente is open to trade visitors only.

### How much is an Ambiente ticket?

When we checked, an online 1-day ticket with Info Service was EUR 29 and a season ticket was EUR 55. Buy online in the official ticket shop and check current prices there.

### Where do I find manufacturers at Ambiente?

Look in the Global Sourcing areas, including the new Global Sourcing Dining area. They gather manufacturers that make products to order, including private label.
