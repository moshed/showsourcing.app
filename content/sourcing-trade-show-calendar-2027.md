## How to pick your shows

1. **Start from your categories.** Electronics buyers: CES, Hong Kong in April and October, IFA. Home, gift and décor: Las Vegas Market, Ambiente, NY NOW. Small daily goods: Yiwu Fair, and the Canton Fair phase that matches your category.
2. **Decide where you want to buy.** Factory-direct private label is easiest at the Asia shows and in the sourcing areas at CES, IFA Global Markets and Ambiente. Finished brands for resale are easiest at Las Vegas Market and NY NOW.
3. **Stack shows that run together.** In April and October, the Hong Kong shows and Canton Fair overlap. Many buyers do Hong Kong first, then Guangzhou, and add Yiwu in October.
4. **Watch early prices.** CES, for example, raises its pass price after 30 November.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the biggest sourcing trade shows in 2027?

For electronics: CES (6-9 January, Las Vegas), the HKTDC Hong Kong Electronics Fair (13-16 April) and IFA (3-7 September, Berlin). For home and gift: Las Vegas Market (24-28 January), Ambiente (29 January - 2 February, Frankfurt) and NY NOW (31 January - 2 February, New York). Canton Fair in Guangzhou runs every spring and autumn.

### When is Canton Fair 2027?

The 141st Canton Fair is the spring 2027 session. The official site had not published its dates on 27 October 2026. Spring sessions usually run from mid-April to early May in three phases.

### Which trade shows are in Hong Kong in 2027?

The HKTDC Hong Kong Electronics Fair Spring Edition runs 13-16 April 2027 at HKCEC. Global Sources usually runs shows at AsiaWorld-Expo in April and October, but had not published 2027 dates when we checked.

### Which shows are best for private label sourcing?

The Asia shows (Canton Fair, Hong Kong, Yiwu) and the sourcing areas at Western shows: IFA Global Markets in Berlin and the Global Sourcing areas at Ambiente in Frankfurt. That is where manufacturers who make products for other brands exhibit.
