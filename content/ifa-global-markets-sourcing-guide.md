**IFA** is a big consumer electronics and home appliance show at **Messe Berlin**. Next to the brand halls, **IFA Global Markets** is a strictly B2B sourcing event for **OEM and ODM** suppliers: factories that make products for other brands.

Facts on this page come from ifa-berlin.com, checked on 27 October 2026. Check the official site for 2027 details.

## The short answer

- **IFA 2027 dates:** **3-7 September 2027**.
- **Venue:** Messe Berlin, Messedamm 22, 14055 Berlin, Germany.
- **IFA Global Markets:** B2B sourcing for OEM and ODM. In 2026 it ran 5-7 September in Halls 26a-c. The 2027 dates were not published when we checked.
- **Hours (2026 pattern):** trade visitors 9:30-18:00. Private visitors also attend, with shorter hours. Check 2027 hours.
- **Questions:** support@ifa-management.com.

## IFA versus IFA Global Markets

| | IFA main show | IFA Global Markets |
|---|---|---|
| Who exhibits | Brands showing finished products | Manufacturers, OEM and ODM suppliers |
| Who visits | Trade and the public | Trade buyers only (B2B) |
| Good for | Seeing trends and new products | Finding a factory for your own brand |

If you build a private label line, Global Markets is the part to plan around.

## Plan your days

1. **Check the Global Markets dates.** In 2026 it ran three of IFA's five days.
2. **Shortlist suppliers** from the official exhibitor list. Note hall and booth.
3. **Do Global Markets first,** while you have energy for detailed talks.
4. **Use the brand halls** to see what is selling, then take those ideas back to the factories.
5. **Go early on public days.** Brand halls get crowded once private visitors arrive.

## At each booth

- Ask for the factory's main products and its biggest customers' markets.
- Ask MOQ, lead time, price term and who owns the tooling for a custom product.
- Ask for CE and other certifications for your market.
- Photograph the product and the label, and swap cards.
- See [25 questions to ask suppliers](/blog/questions-to-ask-suppliers-at-trade-shows/).

## After the show

IFA is in early September, so a follow-up within a week still leaves time for Q4 orders. See [how to follow up](/blog/follow-up-with-suppliers-after-trade-show/). Compare quotes on landed cost: [landed cost from FOB to LDP](/blog/landed-cost-fob-to-ldp/).

## Frequently asked questions

### When is IFA 2027?

IFA Berlin 2027 runs 3-7 September 2027 at Messe Berlin.

### What is IFA Global Markets?

IFA Global Markets is IFA's strictly B2B sourcing event for OEM and ODM suppliers. In 2026 it ran 5-7 September in Halls 26a-c at Messe Berlin.

### Is IFA open to the public?

Yes, partly. IFA admits both trade visitors and private visitors, with different hours. IFA Global Markets is B2B only.

### Is IFA good for private label sourcing?

Yes, through IFA Global Markets. That is where OEM and ODM manufacturers exhibit. The main IFA halls are mostly brands showing their own products.
