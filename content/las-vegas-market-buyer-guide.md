**Las Vegas Market** is a trade market for **furniture, home décor and gift**. The winter market is the big one. It fills a four-building campus with permanent showrooms and temporary exhibits.

Facts on this page come from lasvegasmarket.com, checked on 27 October 2026. Check the official site for hours and badge details.

## The short answer

- **Dates:** **24-28 January 2027**.
- **What:** furniture, home décor and gift. The site lists over 3,500 brands.
- **Where:** a four-building campus in Las Vegas.
- **Who can go:** trade only. Retailers, buyers, designers, architects and others in the home furnishings, gift and design trades.
- **Registration:** online, with proof of trade credentials. Approval can take up to a week after your documents arrive.
- **App:** the ANDMORE Markets app (ANDMORE runs the market).

## Register early

Trade approval is not instant. Register two to three weeks before the market, and have your documents ready, for example a business license, a resale certificate or a recent invoice. The registration page lists what it accepts. Some badge types may have a fee.

## How the market works

Las Vegas Market mixes two kinds of space:

- **Permanent showrooms.** Brands keep these all year. Many show their full line and write orders on the spot.
- **Temporary exhibits.** Smaller and newer companies take booths for the market only.

Showrooms are spread over many floors in several buildings. Without a plan, you lose hours in elevators.

## Plan your days

1. **Use the official app and exhibitor search** to list the showrooms you must see. Note building, floor and room.
2. **Group your list by building and floor.** Work top to bottom, one building at a time.
3. **Book appointments** with key showrooms. Busy showrooms can't give walk-ins much time.
4. **Do temporary exhibits on a separate day.** New suppliers are often there.
5. **Keep the last day for re-visits** and for writing orders after you compare.

## At each showroom

- Ask the wholesale price, the opening order minimum and the reorder minimum.
- Ask about market specials. Many showrooms offer show-only terms.
- Ask the lead time and the freight terms. Furniture freight can change your margin a lot.
- Photograph each piece with its tag, and note the room number.
- See [25 questions to ask suppliers](/blog/questions-to-ask-suppliers-at-trade-shows/).

## After the market

Compare what you saw on the same terms: landed cost, lead time and minimums. See [how to compare supplier quotes](/blog/compare-supplier-quotes/). Follow up within a week on everything you did not order at the market. See [how to follow up](/blog/follow-up-with-suppliers-after-trade-show/).

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Las Vegas Market 2027?

The Winter 2027 Las Vegas Market runs 24-28 January 2027.

### Is Las Vegas Market open to the public?

No. It is for trade professionals in home furnishings, gift and design, such as retailers, buyers, designers and architects. You must register and show trade credentials.

### How long does Las Vegas Market registration take?

Approval can take up to one week after all your documents arrive. Register two to three weeks before the market.

### What is sold at Las Vegas Market?

Furniture, home décor and gift. The official site lists over 3,500 brands, in permanent showrooms and temporary exhibits.
