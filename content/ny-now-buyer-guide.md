**NY NOW** is a market for **gift, home and handmade** products, held at the **Javits Center** in New York. Independent retailers, museum shops, boutiques and department store buyers come to find new lines.

Facts on this page come from nynow.com, checked on 27 October 2026. Check the official site for hours and registration details.

## The short answer

- **Dates:** Winter 2027, **31 January - 2 February 2027**.
- **Venue:** Javits Center, New York.
- **Who can go:** trade buyers. You register online and pass a qualification step.
- **Returning buyers:** if you registered for Summer 2025, Winter 2026 or Summer 2026, you are pre-approved when you use the same email.
- **Questions:** registration@nynow.com.

## What is on the floor

| Area | What you find |
|---|---|
| Gift & Lifestyle | Novelty gifts, fragrance and personal care, jewelry and fashion accessories, stationery and publishing, baby, toys |
| Home | Tabletop and housewares, textiles, decorative accessories, garden, area rugs |
| Handmade | Designer/Maker and Global Design |
| NOTED | The Greeting Card Expo |

## Plan your days

Three days is short. Plan before you go.

1. **Register early** with your business email, so the qualification step is done before the show.
2. **Use the exhibitor search** to list your must-see booths by area.
3. **Day one:** must-see booths and new brands in Handmade, before small makers sell out of samples or show specials.
4. **Day two:** walk the areas you buy from, one at a time.
5. **Day three:** re-visits and orders.

## At each booth

- Ask the wholesale price, the opening order minimum and the reorder minimum.
- Ask about show specials and payment terms. Many smaller makers offer net 30 to repeat buyers only.
- Ask the lead time, especially for handmade products.
- Ask if they sell to stores near you. Some limit how many stores carry a line in one area.
- Photograph each product with its booth number, and take the line sheet.

See [25 questions to ask suppliers](/blog/questions-to-ask-suppliers-at-trade-shows/).

## After the show

Sort your notes on the train home. Then send follow-ups within a week to the lines you did not order at the show. See [how to follow up](/blog/follow-up-with-suppliers-after-trade-show/) and [how to compare supplier quotes](/blog/compare-supplier-quotes/).

### When is NY NOW 2027?

NY NOW Winter 2027 runs 31 January - 2 February 2027 at the Javits Center in New York.

### Who can attend NY NOW?

Trade buyers, such as independent retailers, designers, museum shops, department stores and boutiques. You register online and pass a qualification step.

### Do I need to qualify again for NY NOW?

If you registered for Summer 2025, Winter 2026 or Summer 2026, you are pre-approved when you register with the same email.
