The **HKTDC Hong Kong Electronics Fair** is one of the biggest electronics sourcing shows in Asia. It runs twice a year, in April and October, at the **Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre (HKCEC)** in Wan Chai.

Facts on this page come from the HKTDC website, checked on 27 October 2026. Dates and hours can change, so check the official fair page before you book.

## The short answer

- **Next edition:** Spring Edition, **13-16 April 2027**.
- **Last edition:** Autumn Edition, 13-16 October 2026, held together with electronicAsia.
- **Venue:** HKCEC, 1 Expo Drive, Wan Chai, Hong Kong.
- **Who can go:** trade buyers. Pre-register online through the fair's visitor page.
- **Badge:** HKTDC uses an e-Badge, shown in the HKTDC Marketplace App.
- **Online sourcing:** Click2Match business matching runs 6-23 April 2027, and the hktdc.com Sourcing platform runs 13 March to 12 May 2027.

## What you will find

HKTDC describes the fair as a show for "leading-edge electronics products". The fair groups part of the floor into themed zones, including:

- Hall of Fame
- Startup Zone
- RoboPark
- Adventure Hub
- Immersive Experience Zone

The full product category list is on the HKTDC fair page. Read it before the show and mark the categories you buy.

## Opening hours

At the Autumn 2026 edition, the fair opened 9:30 to 18:30 on the first three days and 9:30 to 17:00 on the last day. The Spring 2027 hours were not published when we checked. Plan on a shorter last day.

## Plan your days

1. **Pre-register online.** It saves time on the first morning, when lines are longest.
2. **Use the exhibitor search** to make a shortlist. Note the hall and booth number of each supplier.
3. **Book Click2Match meetings** before you fly. Suppliers with a meeting give you their full attention.
4. **Walk your must-see booths on day one.** Popular samples run out.
5. **Keep the last afternoon free** for second visits to your shortlist.
6. **Check the Global Sources show dates.** Global Sources often runs electronics shows at AsiaWorld-Expo in the same weeks. See our [Hong Kong shows guide](/blog/hong-kong-sourcing-shows-guide/).

## At each booth

- Ask the price, the minimum order quantity (MOQ) and the price term (FOB, EXW or other). See [25 questions to ask suppliers](/blog/questions-to-ask-suppliers-at-trade-shows/).
- Ask which certifications the product has for your market, for example FCC or CE.
- Photograph the product, the label and the price sheet.
- Swap business cards, and write the booth number on the card or in your notes.
- Note what makes this supplier different. At an electronics fair, ten booths can show the same earbuds.

## After the fair

Electronics suppliers move fast. Send your follow-up within a week while they still remember you. Then compare quotes on a landed-cost basis, not on the FOB price alone. See [how to compare supplier quotes](/blog/compare-supplier-quotes/) and [landed cost from FOB to LDP](/blog/landed-cost-fob-to-ldp/).

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the Hong Kong Electronics Fair 2027?

The Spring Edition runs 13-16 April 2027 at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre. The Autumn Edition is usually held in October. The 2026 Autumn Edition ran 13-16 October.

### Where is the Hong Kong Electronics Fair held?

At the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre (HKCEC), 1 Expo Drive, Wan Chai, on Hong Kong Island. The nearest MTR stations are Exhibition Centre and Wan Chai.

### How do I register for the Hong Kong Electronics Fair?

Use the visitor pre-registration link on the HKTDC fair page. HKTDC issues an e-Badge that you show in the HKTDC Marketplace App.

### Is the Hong Kong Electronics Fair the same as the Global Sources electronics show?

No. HKTDC runs the Hong Kong Electronics Fair at HKCEC in Wan Chai. Global Sources runs its own consumer electronics shows at AsiaWorld-Expo near the airport. They often run in the same weeks, so many buyers visit both.
