HubSpot is one of the best-known CRMs. It is built for **selling**: contacts, companies, deals and a sales pipeline. Show Sourcing is built for **buying**: products, quotes, MOQ and landed cost, linked to the vendor who quoted them.

Facts about HubSpot come from its CRM Starter page, checked on October 20, 2026. HubSpot often runs promotions, so see [hubspot.com](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm/starter) for current prices.

## The short answer

- Choose **HubSpot** to manage customers and a sales pipeline.
- Choose **Show Sourcing** to manage the products and suppliers you source. Many companies use both: HubSpot for the customers they sell to, Show Sourcing for the factories they buy from.

## Where HubSpot is stronger

- **Sales.** Pipelines, deals, email sync and sequences.
- **Integrations**, with thousands of apps.
- **A free tier** for two users.

## Where Show Sourcing is stronger

- **Product records** with photos and quotes, not contact records with notes.
- **Landed cost** and quote comparison.
- **Made for the show floor**: fast capture, card scan, offline.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use HubSpot to track suppliers?

You can store suppliers as companies and contacts. HubSpot is designed for sales pipelines, so product photos, price breaks, MOQ and landed cost need custom setup or are not available.

### Is Show Sourcing a CRM?

Yes, a sourcing CRM. It manages vendors and the products they quote, rather than customers and deals. It is not a sales CRM.
