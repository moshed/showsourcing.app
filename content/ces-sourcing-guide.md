**CES** is the big consumer technology show in Las Vegas every January. Most visitors come for brand launches. Buyers can also use it to find suppliers: many manufacturers exhibit, and you can meet them without flying to Asia.

Facts on this page come from ces.tech, checked on 27 October 2026. Prices and rules can change, so check the official registration page.

## The short answer

- **Dates:** **6-9 January 2027**. CES Unveiled, the press preview, is on 4 January.
- **Venues:** the Las Vegas Convention Center (LVCC) campus and the Venetian Expo. CES Foundry runs 6-8 January at Fontainebleau.
- **Who can go:** trade only. You must be 18 or older and work in the consumer technology industry.
- **Pass prices (when we checked):** Standard Pass $149 until 30 November, then $349. Premium Pass $1,450, then $1,750.
- **Proof:** you must show industry credentials, such as a business website, an invoice or proof of employment.

## Register early

1. **Register before 30 November** for the lower price.
2. **Upload a headshot.** Without one, CES does not print your badge.
3. **Pick up your own badge** with a government photo ID. Badges are not mailed.
4. **Save the QR code** from your confirmation. It makes pickup faster.

## Where suppliers are

CES spreads over several buildings. Large brands take the big halls. Manufacturers, component makers and smaller companies are often in other halls and at the Venetian Expo. Use the official exhibitor list and floor plans to find:

- Companies that list OEM or ODM services (they make products for other brands).
- Accessory makers for the products you sell.
- Startups with new products that need distribution.

The walk between the LVCC and the Venetian takes time. Plan one area per half day.

## Plan your days

1. **Shortlist exhibitors by category** from the official list. Note the hall and booth.
2. **Email your top ten before the show** and ask for a meeting time.
3. **Day one:** your meetings and must-see booths.
4. **Day two and three:** walk the halls by area.
5. **Last day:** second visits. Many exhibitors pack up early on the last afternoon.

## At each booth

- Ask if they sell to retailers, make private label, or only sell their own brand. At CES, many booths only want distributors.
- Ask the MOQ, lead time and the price term. Many CES suppliers quote FOB from Asia even at a US show.
- Ask which certifications the product has, for example FCC and UL.
- Photograph the product and the label, and swap cards. See [25 questions to ask suppliers](/blog/questions-to-ask-suppliers-at-trade-shows/).

## After CES

Follow up the next week, before the Chinese New Year break slows factories down. See [how to follow up after a trade show](/blog/follow-up-with-suppliers-after-trade-show/). Compare quotes on landed cost: [landed cost from FOB to LDP](/blog/landed-cost-fob-to-ldp/).

## Frequently asked questions

### When is CES 2027?

CES 2027 runs 6-9 January 2027 in Las Vegas. CES Unveiled is on 4 January 2027.

### Can anyone go to CES?

No. CES is a trade event for people 18 or older who work in the consumer technology industry. You must show credentials, such as a business website, an invoice or proof of employment.

### How much does a CES 2027 pass cost?

When we checked, the Standard Pass was $149 until 30 November 2026 and $349 after that. The Premium Pass was $1,450, then $1,750. Check ces.tech for current prices.

### Is CES good for finding suppliers?

Yes, if you plan. Many manufacturers and component makers exhibit, often away from the big brand halls. Use the official exhibitor list to find companies that offer OEM, ODM or private label, and book meetings before the show.
