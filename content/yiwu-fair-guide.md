The **China Yiwu International Commodities Fair**, usually called the **Yiwu Fair**, is a large trade fair for everyday consumer goods. It is held at the **Yiwu International Expo Center** in Yiwu, Zhejiang province, the same city as the huge Yiwu wholesale market.

Facts on this page come from the official Yiwu Fair website (en.yiwufair.com), checked on 27 October 2026. Check the site for the next dates before you book.

## The short answer

- **Last edition:** the 32nd fair, **21-24 October 2026**, with a public day on 25 October.
- **Hours (2026):** trade days 8:30-17:00; public day 8:30-14:00.
- **Venue:** Yiwu International Expo Center, Yiwu, Zhejiang, China.
- **Size (2026):** about 3,000 exhibitors on 110,000 square meters, and about 180,000 buyers expected.
- **Badge:** free for trade buyers.
- **2027 dates:** not yet published when we checked.

## What you will find

The official site lists these categories:

- Daily consumer goods
- Hardware tools and architectural hardware
- Electronic appliances
- Toys
- Sports and outdoor leisure goods
- Cultural and office supplies
- Trade services

Prices are often low and MOQs (minimum order quantities) are often small. That makes it good for testing many products in small runs.

## How to get the free buyer badge

The official badge page lists these ways:

1. **Pre-register online** at buyer.yiwufair.com.
2. **Pre-register on WeChat** through the fair's mini-program.
3. **Register on site** at the Buyers Registration Spot. Bring your passport and a business card.
4. **Overseas buyers with a Canton Fair trade badge** can get a free Yiwu Fair badge at the Overseas Buyers Registration Spot.

Pre-registering saves time on the first morning.

## Plan your days

1. **Pick your categories first.** The fair is big, and most booths sell many small items.
2. **Give the fair two or three days**, then one or two days for the Yiwu market if you go there too.
3. **Combine it with Canton Fair** if the dates fit. Yiwu is about six hours from Guangzhou by high-speed train, or a short flight. See our [Canton Fair checklist](/blog/canton-fair-buyer-prep-checklist/).
4. **Book a hotel early.** Fair weeks fill the city. The official site has hotel and transport pages.

## At each booth

- Photograph every item you like, with its price tag or price sheet in the same photo.
- Ask the MOQ, the carton quantity and the price term. Prices at small-goods booths are often EXW (at the factory) or ex-warehouse, not FOB.
- Ask if the booth is a factory or a trading company. Both are common in Yiwu.
- Swap business cards and note the booth number. Many booths look alike by day two.
- See [25 questions to ask suppliers](/blog/questions-to-ask-suppliers-at-trade-shows/).

## After the fair

Small items mean many suppliers and many quotes. Sort them on the same evening while you still remember each booth. Then compare them on landed cost: small goods often cost more to ship and clear than to buy. See [landed cost from FOB to LDP](/blog/landed-cost-fob-to-ldp/) and [how to follow up](/blog/follow-up-with-suppliers-after-trade-show/).

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the Yiwu Fair?

The 32nd China Yiwu International Commodities Fair ran 21-24 October 2026, with a public day on 25 October. The 2027 dates were not published when we checked. See en.yiwufair.com.

### Is the Yiwu Fair free for buyers?

Yes. Trade buyers get a free badge. Pre-register at buyer.yiwufair.com or on WeChat, or register on site with your passport and business card.

### Can I use my Canton Fair badge at the Yiwu Fair?

Overseas buyers who hold a Canton Fair trade badge can get a free Yiwu Fair badge at the Overseas Buyers Registration Spot.

### Is the Yiwu Fair the same as the Yiwu market?

No. The Yiwu Fair is a trade fair at the Yiwu International Expo Center on set dates. The Yiwu International Trade City is a permanent wholesale market that is open most of the year. Many buyers visit both on one trip.
