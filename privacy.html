Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Introduction

Show Sourcing ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our app and website.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email, name and profile details.
  • Your catalog: the products, photos, prices, vendors and notes you add.
  • Business cards (optional): scanning vendor business cards is optional. If you scan one, we keep the card photo and the contact details on it.
  • Location (optional): only if you turn on Team Radar.
  • Usage and device information: app version, device type, and a record of actions in your account (for example, adding or deleting a product). We use this for support, security and to resolve disputes.
  • Quote forms: if a vendor fills in a quote form you send them, we collect what they enter and pass it to your team.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to run the service: to sync your data, let your team work together, send notifications, provide support, and keep accounts secure. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored with a trusted cloud provider and is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to you and the team members you invite.

Service Providers and AI

Much of the app's intelligence runs on your own device, such as reading text in photos and turning voice notes into text.

We also use trusted service providers to run the app: cloud hosting, payments, maps, push notifications, and AI services that help read business cards, fill in product details and search tariffs. They get only the data needed for that task and may not use it for anything else. Your photos are not sent to AI services.

  • Business card reading is optional. You can turn off AI card reading in Settings. Cards are then read on your device only.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card number.
  • Your own AI assistant: if you choose to connect one, it receives the catalog data you ask it about, under its own privacy terms.

When We Share Data

We share data only with the team members you invite, with the service providers above to run the app, or when the law requires it.

Location Data

The Team Radar feature uses your location only while the app is in the foreground and you have enabled location sharing. You can disable location sharing at any time through Ghost Mode or device settings. We do not track your location when the app is closed.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
